ExTrace Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

ExTrace is a budgeting application designed to help users track their income, expenses, savings goals, and daily budgets.

Information We Collect

ExTrace stores budgeting information entered by the user, such as income, fixed expenses, savings goals, and expense records. This information is primarily stored locally on the user's device.

Notifications

If notifications are enabled, ExTrace may send budget reminders, expense reminders, and other budgeting-related notifications. Notifications can be disabled through the app's settings.

Data Sharing

ExTrace does not sell or share your budgeting information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Security

ExTrace is designed to keep budgeting information stored locally on the user's device. Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their device.

Children's Privacy

ExTrace is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
